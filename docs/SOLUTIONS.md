# Creator's Solution Guide — Case File 001

> **SPOILERS.** This file explains every puzzle and every answer. It is for the
> person giving the gift, not the person receiving it. It is not part of the
> deployed website (only the `site/` folder is deployed).

---

## The story in one minute

**The false lead.** A small archive, the Wrenfield, reports that Item 0047 (a
walnut music box) has vanished from a locked display case in Reading Room 4.

**What really happened.** There never was a music box. Over six nights (8–13
November) a visitor who signs only with a diamond **◇** left one small thing in
the room each night: *a sound* (a hum), *a name* (the label "0047"), *a note*
(under the cabinet), *a shape* (the outline on the velvet), *a box* (under the
velvet), and *a question* (the "missing" report that starts the investigation).

The archivist saw an empty case and called it a loss. The night custodian heard
the hum every night and was the only one who listened. Everything was left for
whoever would follow it to the end, and that person is the player.

**The reveal.** *"The object was never the point."* The final report says Item
0047 "is in this room". In the glass of the empty case, the player sees their
own reflection.

**The closing lines** (exactly as specified):

> "You spent all this time looking for something hidden."
> *(pause)*
> "Maybe that was the point."
> "The things worth noticing are rarely the loudest things in the room."
> **CASE CLOSED**

**The hidden layer.** That last line is also the key to the Deep Archive: six
faint pencil marks, one in each case, spell **NOTICE**.

## Case 001 — The Object

**Evidence:** Incident report · Catalogue card 0047 · Photograph of display
case 3 · *(hidden)* The curator's note

**Path:**
1. Open the photograph and tap around it. Each hotspot gives a detail, and the
   details matter later:
   - *The velvet:* a sharp outline that looks **made, not left behind**.
   - *The label:* "ITEM 0047", engraved **ON LOAN**.
   - *The lock:* polished, opened often.
   - *Under the cabinet:* a folded card → **unlocks Exhibit 1-D, the curator's note**.
   - *The glass:* "Someone is standing where you are standing now, looking in."
     (This foreshadows the ending.)
2. The note is seven handwritten lines. Its last line tells you how to read it:
   *"Trust the beginnings, not the endings."*

```
P lease don't call it stolen, not just no w
R eading rooms like this keep no alib i
E very empty case is keeping a secre t
S ome things are only kept by being give n
E ven dust can tell the tim e
N othing here was taken. Look at what remain s
T rust the beginnings, not the ending s
```

First letters → **PRESENT**. The curator is saying the object is *present*, not
missing. In Case 005 it also turns out to mean *a present*.

*(The last letters spell **WITNESS**. That is the Case 005 answer, hidden in plain
sight from the first minute. If a sharp player types WITNESS here they get a
nudge: "That word is in there too, but not yet.")*

**Facts the player will need later:** the music box plays **five notes**
(catalogue card) and it was in **display case 3** (report).

**Hints:** (1) look harder at the photograph → (2) there's something under the
cabinet → (3) read the first letter of each line → (reveal) PRESENT.

**Near misses with a nudge:** WITNESS, STOLEN, MISSING, ON LOAN.

**Margin mark 1 — "N":** a faint pencil letter in the photograph's bottom-right
white border.

## Case 002 — The Pattern

**Evidence:** Six visitor slips signed ◇ · Camera 2 stills, 8–13 November

**Path:**
1. The slips all say *Name: ◇*. Their **dates are washed out**, and only the minutes
   differ: 21:20, 21:05, 21:12, 21:14, 21:09, 21:19 (shown shuffled). E. Varga's
   memo says: "Only the minutes change."
2. The camera stills are **dated** (08–13 NOV) but the time readout is broken.
   Each frame shows a **wall clock**; tap it to zoom in.
3. Read each clock in date order:

| Date | Clock | Minute | Letter |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 Nov | 9:12 | 12 | L |
| 9 Nov | 9:09 | 9 | I |
| 10 Nov | 9:19 | 19 | S |
| 11 Nov | 9:20 | 20 | T |
| 12 Nov | 9:05 | 5 | E |
| 13 Nov | 9:14 | 14 | N |

→ **LISTEN** (A=1 … Z=26).

**The built-in trap:** L-I-S-T-E-N also spells **SILENT, ENLIST, TINSEL, INLETS**.
Anyone who skips the stills and guesses an anagram is told *"The right letters in
the wrong order. Put the nights in sequence."*

**Image clue for observant players:** the stills show the case changing: no label
on the 8th, **label from the 9th**, a white card corner under the cabinet from the
10th, **outline on the velvet from the 11th**, velvet slightly raised from the
12th (the box is under it). These are things being added, not taken. Case 003
confirms each one.

**Hints:** (1) compare the slips → (2) only the minutes change, 1–26; the stills
give the dates → (3) read the clocks in date order, 1 = A → (reveal) LISTEN.

**Margin mark 2 — "O":** on the back of the **21:09** slip (turn it over).

## Case 003 — The Witness

**Evidence:** Interview tape (T. Reyl, night custodian) · Reyl's night duty log ·
Custodian's handbook p. 14 (Morse code table)

**The contradiction:** Reyl has dusted case 3 every night for eleven years and
**it has always been empty**. He never saw a music box. The label "turned up on the
ninth"; a mark appeared on the velvet on the eleventh; and every night at about
21:10 he heard **a hum from inside the case, short and long**. On the 12th he
recorded it.

**Path:**
1. Play the tape. At 01:11 it plays the recording: a soft hum in short and long
   pulses. You can also play just the hum, watch the **waveform** (short bars and
   long bars), or switch on **"Show pulses as text"**.
2. Decode with the handbook page:

```
.-..  ---  ---  -.-   /   ..-  -.  -..  .  .-.
 L     O    O    K          U    N   D   E   R
```

→ **LOOK UNDER**

**Accessibility:** the whole tape has a transcript, the pulses are drawn as a
waveform, and they can be shown as dots and dashes. The puzzle never depends on
hearing.

**Hints:** (1) play the whole tape → (2) it's Morse; use the handbook → (3) short =
dot, long = dash, gaps separate letters and words → (reveal) LOOK UNDER.

**Near misses:** UNDER ("there is one before it"), LOOK ("keep listening").

**Margin mark 3 — "T":** on the cassette's label, bottom-right corner.

## Case 004 — The Missing Piece

**Evidence:** Steel lockbox tagged 0047 · *(after opening)* Accession slip ·
Cipher wheel · Brass winding key

### Part 1: the lockbox (multi-clue)

Four paper tags, one per dial, each with a symbol and a question:

| Dial | Tag | Question | Answer | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | ♪ note | How many? | **5** | Catalogue card: "a melody of five notes" (001) |
| II | ◇ diamond | How many nights? | **6** | Six ◇ slips / six stills, 8–13 Nov (002) |
| III | label | Which night did I appear? | **9** | Reyl: "The label turned up on the ninth" (003) |
| IV | display case | Which case? | **3** | Incident report: display case 3 (001) |

Combination: **5 · 6 · 9 · 3**. The notebook automatically records each of these
facts as the player meets them, so nobody has to remember everything.

### Part 2: the cipher

The accession slip (the record that was "missing" from the catalogue) reads:

```
Contents:     SRI UYMIX XLMRK
Filed under:  MRHIB
Stamp:        CIPHER · SET THE WHEEL TO THE ROOM
```

"The room" is **Reading Room 4**, so set the wheel to **4**. Find each letter on
the inner ring and read the outer ring (each letter shifts back four):

- SRI UYMIX XLMRK → **ONE QUIET THING**
- MRHIB → **INDEX**

The wheel also shows a two-row strip (outer/inner) for easier reading on small
screens.

**Hints change once the box is open:** before opening they lead to 5-6-9-3; after
opening they lead to setting 4 and the decoded letters → (reveal) INDEX.

**Near misses:** ONE QUIET THING ("where was it filed?"), MRHIB ("still in
cipher"), 5693 ("that opens the box").

**Safety net:** if the player gets the key from the hint reveal without opening
the box, the game still gives them the winding key they need in Case 006.

**Margin mark 4 — "I":** inside the lockbox lid (visible once open).

## Case 005 — The Archive

**Evidence:** The card index (six drawers) · *(after card 0047)* Ultraviolet lamp

**Path:**
1. Drawers are labelled by catalogue number. Open **0041–0048** and turn over
   **card 0047**. The reverse explains the curator's marks:
   - ◇ **deposited** (left in our keeping)
   - △ **look again** (the small triangle on the catalogue card in 001)
   - ○ **listen** (the small circle on the tape label in 003)

   The player also receives **an ultraviolet lamp**.
2. **Earlier clues gain a different meaning.** Every document read so far now has
   a **Lamp** switch: sweep the beam, or flood the page. Hidden ink appears:

| Evidence | Under the lamp |
|---|---|
| Incident report | "missing" crossed out, "present" written above. *"They saw an empty space and called it a loss. Most people do."* |
| Catalogue card | *"Catalogued by me. Acquired by no one."* |
| Photograph | The outline glows with a ◇ and the word *deposited* |
| **Curator's note** | **The last letter of every line glows: W-I-T-N-E-S-S.** *"I lied about the endings."* |
| Visitor slips | Dates restored, and each slip says what was left: a sound, a name, a note, a shape, a box, a question |
| Tape label | *"Thank you for listening, T."* |
| Reyl's log | *"He noticed, every night. Nobody asked him."* |
| Accession slip | *"The missing piece was never in the box."* |
| Card 0047 | *"The first note has two edges. You've only read one."* |

3. The key question is *"The first note has two edges. What does the other edge
   say?"* → **WITNESS**.

Read together: **PRESENT WITNESS**. Typing "present witness" is accepted too.

**Hints:** (1) find card 0047 and turn it over → (2) use the lamp on earlier
evidence, starting with the first note → (3) the last letter of each line glows →
(reveal) WITNESS.

**Margin mark 5 — "C":** on the back of **card 0048** ("Envelope, empty. Labelled
'later'.").

## Case 006 — The Truth

**Evidence:** Final report (unsigned, **dated today**, using the player's own
device date) · Reading Room 4, 21:12

**Path:**
1. Read the final report. It explains everything and ends: *"Item 0047 is not a
   music box. It is not missing. It is in this room. Conclusion: The object was
   never the point."* The investigator's name is redacted. After the case closes
   it reads **"the one who noticed"**.
2. Open Reading Room 4 and **wind the key**. Five notes play from the empty case.
   The glass shows *"the lamp, the empty velvet, and you, looking in."*
3. **Close the case.** This starts the full-screen finale, then **CASE CLOSED**.

**Margin mark 6 — "E":** bottom-right of the final report, next to "unsigned".

## The Secret Deep Archive

**How it unlocks:** after CASE CLOSED, a very faint ◇ sits at the bottom of the
closed screen (and of the case file). A faint line above it reads *"Some things in
this file were quieter than others."* Tapping the ◇ opens a locked drawer with six
letter wheels. The notebook's **Margins** section shows the marks found so far, in
case order: **N O T I C E**. Missing letters show as dots, so four or five marks
are usually enough to guess.

**Door hints:** (1) each case has one faint pencil mark → (2) exactly where each
one is → (3) read them in case order → (reveal) NOTICE.

**Contents:**
- **Item 0047, as described:** the music box, made at last. Wind it to hear the
  longer melody; the lid opens: *"Received: one investigator. Condition:
  excellent. No longer on loan."*
- **Slip 7:** a seventh ◇ slip dated today at the current time. *Purpose: "To see if
  you'd come."*
- **Unused documents:** Case File 000 (a rejected "stolen painting" draft: *"Too
  loud."*), questions nobody asked, and a rejected label (*"FOR THE ONE WHO
  NOTICES. Too obvious."*).
- **Hidden visual, Plate 7:** a nine-hour exposure of the dark room. Press *Develop*
  and a light trail appears: someone drew **a wren** in the air with a lamp.
- **Curator's notes:** how and why the case was built.
- **The final subtle message:** printed very faintly at the bottom of the
  curator's notes, and glowing under the lamp:
  > *P.S. You were the thing worth noticing.*

  You can change this line and add a dedication. See [CUSTOMIZING.md](CUSTOMIZING.md).

## If she gets stuck

- Every case has three hints and then an optional reveal of the key. Nobody can
  get permanently stuck.
- The **Notebook** records keys, tools and every fact noticed so far.
- If you're with her, nudge with the hint wording above. It's written to give a
  direction without giving the answer away.
- **Settings → Reset investigation** starts over on that device.
